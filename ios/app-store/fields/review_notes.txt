Maginet is a landscape-only tactics game for iPhone. No app account or sign-in is required.

Free content: Tutorial, campaign levels Basics I–IV, local battles, battles against AI, and the level editor.

Full Game is a permanent non-consumable in-app purchase (zone.evrim.maginet.all). It unlocks the remaining campaign and all online modes. To find the purchase, open Settings → Unlock Full Game. Restore Purchases is also available in Settings. The campaign purchase entry appears at Patterns I, after Basics IV.

The game starts from bundled content. Campaign, local battles, and AI play work offline. Online play requires an internet connection and the Full Game purchase.

The App Store screenshots include campaign content available with Full Game.
