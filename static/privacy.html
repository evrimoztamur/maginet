Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy covers the Maginet iPhone and Android apps, the browser game, and the online game service at maginet.evrim.zone. Maginet is operated by Evrim Öztamur. For privacy questions or support, email maginet@evrim.zone.

In brief: You do not need an account. Local progress stays on your device. Online play sends game information to our server. Apple and Google Play handle in-app purchases on their respective platforms. Maginet has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Local play and saved progress

Maginet stores campaign progress, settings, and levels you create in the app’s local storage or your browser’s site storage. These local saves are not uploaded to our game server unless you use a created level in an online match. The iPhone and Android apps bundle its game content, so campaign, AI battles, and local battles can run without connecting to the game server. The browser game downloads its content from the website.

Online play

The online service assigns a random session ID to distinguish players and associate them with a match. The ID is stored on your device and sent with game requests. It is not your Apple or Google account, email address, or advertising identifier.

To run and synchronize online games, the server processes session IDs, lobby identifiers, team assignments, connection timestamps, match settings, board layouts, and moves. It saves match records containing this information. Lobby and game-state responses include session IDs and game information, and can be accessed by clients using the service. Do not put personal or confidential information in shared game content.

Connecting to the website or online service also exposes your IP address and normal request information to the hosting infrastructure. Standard access and security logs can contain the IP address, request time, requested path, browser or client information, and response status.

Purchases

Apple processes the Full Game purchase and purchase restoration through the App Store. The app checks Apple-verified transaction information to determine whether Full Game is unlocked. The game server does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials, and the app does not send StoreKit transaction records to the game server. Apple handles its own purchase records under Apple’s privacy policy.

On Android, Google Play processes Full Game purchases and restoration. The app verifies signed purchase information locally, acknowledges completed purchases through Google Play, and stores verified ownership in app-private storage encrypted with Android Keystore. This ownership cache is excluded from Android backups. Purchase tokens and signed purchase records are not sent to the game server. Google handles its own purchase records under Google’s privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as an issue description or screenshots. We use this information to respond and help resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

How information is used and shared

We use game information to provide online play, maintain the service, and investigate problems. Connection information may be used to operate and protect the service. Support messages are used to handle your request. Hosting and email providers process information needed to deliver these services.

Maginet does not sell personal information, use advertising identifiers, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

Retention and deletion

  • Local saves: Remain until you clear the app’s or browser’s stored data. Uninstalling the Android app or deleting the iPhone app removes local data from that installation; offloading the iPhone app preserves data. Device or browser backups may retain copies under your backup settings.
  • Online match records: Saved records currently have no automatic expiry. They remain until removed or replaced. Closing the app or finishing a match does not automatically delete a saved server record.
  • Connection logs: The web server is configured to rotate logs daily and keep 14 rotated log files, in addition to the current log. Empty logs are not rotated, so low-traffic logs may cover a longer period. Contact us for information about a particular request.
  • Support correspondence: May remain in our support mailbox after an issue is resolved. You can request deletion, subject to any information we must retain to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.
  • Store purchase records: Are managed by Apple or Google Play. Deleting Maginet does not cancel or erase a store purchase.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, or to object to its use where applicable, email maginet@evrim.zone. For online match records, include the lobby code and approximate date if available. Because Maginet does not require an account or attach your name to a session, we may need additional information to locate the relevant record and verify the request. You may also raise a privacy concern with your local data protection authority.

Your choices

You can use the mobile apps’ offline modes without using the multiplayer service. You can clear local saves through your device or browser settings, and you can choose what to include in a support message. Maginet does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the game or its privacy practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Need a hand?

For help with Maginet, purchases, or restoring Full Game, email maginet@evrim.zone. Include your phone model, iOS or Android version, and a short description of the issue. For browser issues, include your browser name and version.
